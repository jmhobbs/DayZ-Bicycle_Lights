# DayZ Bicycle Lights

Adds the ability to attach a chemlight and/or flashlight to your DayZ Bicycle.

Toggle the flashlight on and off by pressing "L"

## Bicycle Models

The bicycle models are patched versions of the [DayZ-Bicycle](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2971190303) models and are thus not under the MIT License.
